Frequently Asked

Is LuckySevenStudio a gambling app?

No. LuckySevenStudio is a design and composition tool. It contains no wagering, no payouts, no monetary outcomes, and no in-app purchases of virtual currency or value-equivalent items. The 777 motif is used purely as a visual language for composition, color, and rhythm.

Where is my work stored?

Everything you create — Reel Composer saves, Fortune Wheel configurations, Lucky Number history, Symbol selections, Pattern compositions, Visual Studio snapshots, captions, tags, and Statistics — is stored locally on your iPhone. We do not operate a server for this data and cannot recover it for you if the App is uninstalled.

Why isn't my photo import working?

LuckySevenStudio needs permission to access your photo library. Open iOS Settings → LuckySevenStudio → Photos and choose "All Photos," "Selected Photos," or "Add Photos Only." If permission was denied, the App won't be able to import or save images. You can also capture directly using the in-app camera, which uses a different permission.

How do I permanently delete my data?

You can delete any single saved item from inside the App. To remove everything, sign out of nothing (because there is no account) and then uninstall the App. All locally stored content lives inside the App's iOS sandbox and is removed when the App is removed.

Does the App send any telemetry or analytics?

No. The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no tracking pixels. We do not receive telemetry from your device.

How do I restore my collection after reinstalling?

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a backed-up Mac, your App data may be restored from that backup when you reinstall. We do not have a server-side restore. If you decline backups, the App will start fresh.

Can I export my compositions?

Visual Studio snapshots can be saved back to your photo library with captions and tags embedded. Other modules (Reel Composer, Fortune Wheel, Pattern Canvas, Lucky Number, Symbol Studio) currently live inside the App as references for your own creative study.

I have an accessibility request.

We welcome accessibility feedback. Dynamic Type is supported across the App. VoiceOver labels and contrast are tuned as part of every release. If you find a screen or interaction that needs improvement, please email us with details and the iOS version you're using.

Writing a Good Support Email

To help us reply quickly, please include: 1) Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About). 2) LuckySevenStudio version number (found on the Settings tab). 3) A short description of what's happening and what you expected. 4) Screenshots or a screen recording if possible.
