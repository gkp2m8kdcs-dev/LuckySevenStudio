Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Effective Immediately

LuckySevenStudio ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the LuckySevenStudio mobile application (the "App") collects, uses, and discloses. The short version: the App does not collect personal information from you. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

No Account No Tracking No Analytics No Third-Party SDKs

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address).
  • Account credentials. The App has no sign-in, no registration, and no profile.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, session recordings, or crash reports sent to us.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data.
  • Location data, contacts, calendar, microphone, or any background sensor data.
  • Biometric data.

2. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

All content you create within the App — including Reel Composer saves, Fortune Wheel configurations, Lucky Number Visualizer history, Symbol Studio selections, Pattern Canvas compositions, Visual Studio snapshots (with your captions and tags), Collection items, and Statistics — is stored locally on your iPhone.

This data is held inside the App's sandboxed storage and is governed by iOS permissions. It is not uploaded to any server. We do not have access to it, and we cannot retrieve, view, restore, or delete it for you. If you delete the App, this data is removed from your device along with the App.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a local computer, the App's data may be included in those backups in accordance with your iOS backup settings. We do not control, access, or transmit those backups.

3. Photos, Camera, and Photo Library

The Visual Studio feature requires access to the camera or your photo library. When you choose to capture or import a photo, that image is loaded into the App's sandboxed storage for editing and filter processing.

  • We never upload your photos to any server.
  • We do not read, index, or process photos you have not explicitly selected.
  • Camera and photo-library permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → LuckySevenStudio.

4. Permissions the App May Request

  • Camera: To capture photos for the Visual Studio feature. Used only when you tap the camera control.
  • Photo Library / Photo Library Add: To import photos you select, and (if you choose) to save exports back to your library.
  • No other permissions are requested. We do not request location, microphone, contacts, calendar, motion, Bluetooth, Face ID, or any background-tracking permission.

5. Children

The App does not target children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no personal information from anyone, no special handling is required. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through any third-party channel, contact us and we will address it in good faith.

6. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no social plugins, and no external tracking pixels. There is no in-app purchase of currency, tokens, or virtual items, and no real-money wagering or payout functionality.

When you contact us by email, your message and reply are handled directly between you and us at a45984849@gmail.com. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry.

7. Responsible Design Notice

The App is a design and composition tool. It is not a gambling product. No wager is placed, no monetary stake is required, and no prize of monetary value can be won. The 777 motif is used purely as a visual language for composition, color, and rhythm.

If gambling has affected you or someone you know, please contact the National Council on Problem Gambling at 1-800-522-4700 (US), or visit gambleaware.org (UK).

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Portability: Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it directly through the App or by using iOS backup/export tools.
  • Deletion: You can delete saved items from inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all of its local data.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke camera or photo-library access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Do Not Track: We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals by simply not tracking, regardless of signal.

9. International Users

The App does not transfer personal data across borders because it does not transfer personal data at all. By using the App, you understand that your content is stored on your device under your iOS backup configuration.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or best practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect the date of the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted in the "What's New" section of the App's next release.
